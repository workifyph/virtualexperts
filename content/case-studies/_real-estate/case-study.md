## Challenge

Growth opportunities from multiple channels but inconsistent follow-up — leads stalled in inboxes and the agent calendar was reactive.

## Solution

Aligned remote support around lead routing, calendar management, and admin execution. Daily standups kept the funnel moving and the back office tidy.

## Results

Faster lead response, cleaner workflows, and better daily follow-up continuity — agents spent more time in conversations that close.
