## Challenge

Unreliable help managing tenant requests across a growing portfolio — escalations slipped, follow-ups got dropped, and the team was constantly firefighting.

## Solution

Communication support, escalation handling, and structured back-office help — with clear ownership for requests, vendor coordination, and recurring tenant comms.

## Results

More stable day-to-day operations, consistent tenant communication, and significantly less firefighting from the leadership team.
